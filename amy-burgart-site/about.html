About

The marketing manager small businesses wish they'd hired years ago

I'm Amy — a freelance growth marketing manager who steps in as the full marketing department a growing business doesn't have time to build.

My story

I have spent the past 15 years growing businesses through digital marketing, content strategy, and web design through both freelance and in-house work. As a purpose-driven entrepreneur, I enjoy working with small businesses and mission-driven organizations looking to make a positive impact in the world.

I'm not a strategist who hands off a deck and disappears, and I'm not an agency account manager juggling twelve other clients. I'm the person who actually builds the storefront, writes the emails, and reads the analytics — for a small handful of businesses at a time, so the work gets the attention it needs to compound.

Today I work with small businesses — farms, market vendors, chiropractors, massage therapists, and other independent brands — who are excellent at what they do but don't have the time, knowledge, or desire to run marketing themselves. My job is to turn the channels they're already under-using into reliable, repeatable revenue.
